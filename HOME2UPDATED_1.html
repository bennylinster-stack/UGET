OUR MISSION

Firm, dispatchable power from thorium molten salt reactors — manufactured, not improvised.

Building the case before we build the plant.

The regulatory, insurance, and safety work is happening alongside the reactor design — not after it. Every subsystem is modeled against NRC, IAEA, and Euratom frameworks from day one.

The goal is a unit small enough to fabricate off-site and simple enough to license on a predictable timeline, so the first commercial reactor isn't a one-off pilot — it's the first of a line.

We work the way we want power to arrive: quietly, honestly, on time.
